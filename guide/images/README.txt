ONTbarcoder 3.1b — MANUAL.html screenshots
===========================================

Put your screenshots in THIS folder, using EXACTLY the filenames below.
Recommended: PNG, ~1200-1600 px wide (portrait shots can be narrower).

After saving an image, open MANUAL.html, find its <figure>, and change
class="ph"  ->  class=""   to activate it.
(Use class="narrow" instead for tall/portrait screenshots, e.g. the sidebar.)
